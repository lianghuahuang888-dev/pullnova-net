About PullNova

We're on a mission to make every engineering team 10x more productive through AI-native automation.

Our Story

PullNova was born from frustration. As senior engineers at high-growth startups and enterprises, we spent countless hours on repetitive code reviews and debugging CI failures. We built PullNova to handle the mechanical parts of code review — so engineers can focus on architecture, design, and innovation.

Launched in 2025, PullNova now serves over 10,000 engineering teams worldwide, analyzing millions of pull requests and saving teams thousands of hours every month.

Our Values

Developer-First: Every feature is designed to reduce friction, not add it.

AI-Native: We use LLMs where they add real value — not as a gimmick.

Privacy by Default: Zero code retention. On-premise deployment. No training on customer data. Ever.

Transparent Pricing: No hidden fees, no surprise overages, no call-for-pricing games.
