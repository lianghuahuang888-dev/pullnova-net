FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes PullNova different?

Unlike traditional linters, PullNova understands code semantics using large language models. It catches real bugs — not just formatting issues — and explains fixes in plain English.

How fast is setup?

Under 2 minutes via GitHub/GitLab App installation. PullNova begins analyzing pull requests immediately with smart defaults you can customize later.

Can PullNova run on-premise?

Yes. Enterprise plans include full on-premise deployment options with air-gapped support. Your source code never leaves your infrastructure.

Do you support custom rules?

Absolutely. Define rules in YAML, train on your team's historical review decisions, and configure per-repository policies. PullNova learns your team's preferences.
